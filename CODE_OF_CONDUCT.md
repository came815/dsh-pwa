# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in dsh-pwa a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable: harassment, trolling, insulting comments, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the project maintainer via a private channel (open a draft security advisory or email the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile). The maintainer will review and take appropriate action, up to and including a temporary or permanent ban.
